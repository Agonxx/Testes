English Tenses

Todas as estruturas essenciais

Present Tenses
01
Simple Present
S + verb (base) / verb+s (he/she/it)
Hábitos, rotinas, fatos gerais e verdades universais.
+ She works
− She doesn't work
? Does she work?
I drink coffee every morning.
The sun rises in the east.
alwaysusuallyneverevery day
02
Present Continuous
S + am/is/are + verb-ing
Ações acontecendo agora ou em período temporário.
+ She is working
− She isn't working
? Is she working?
I am studying right now.
She is living in London this year.
nowright nowat the momentcurrently
03
Present Perfect
S + have/has + past participle
Experiências, ações passadas com resultado no presente, ou que continuam.
+ She has worked
− She hasn't worked
? Has she worked?
I have never been to Japan.
She has lived here for 10 years.
alreadyyetjusteverneverforsince
04
Present Perfect Continuous
S + have/has + been + verb-ing
Ação que começou no passado e ainda está em andamento (ênfase na duração).
+ She has been working
− She hasn't been working
? Has she been working?
I have been studying for 3 hours.
It has been raining all day.
forsinceall daylately
Past Tenses
05
Simple Past
S + verb-ed / irregular form
Ações concluídas em momento específico no passado.
+ She worked
− She didn't work
? Did she work?
I went to the gym yesterday.
She didn't call me last night.
yesterdaylast yearagoin 2020
06
Past Continuous
S + was/were + verb-ing
Ação em progresso em determinado momento do passado. Frequentemente interrompida por outra ação.
+ She was working
− She wasn't working
? Was she working?
I was sleeping when he called.
They were studying all night.
whilewhenat that momentall night
07
Past Perfect
S + had + past participle
Ação que aconteceu antes de outra ação no passado. O "passado do passado".
+ She had worked
− She hadn't worked
? Had she worked?
When I arrived, she had already left.
He hadn't eaten before the meeting.
alreadybeforeafterby the timewhen
08
Past Perfect Continuous
S + had + been + verb-ing
Ação que estava em progresso por um período, antes de outra ação no passado.
+ She had been working
− She hadn't been working
? Had she been working?
She had been crying before I arrived.
They had been waiting for 2 hours.
forsincebeforewhen
Future Tenses
09
Simple Future — will
S + will + verb (base)
Decisões espontâneas, previsões e promessas no futuro.
+ She will work
− She won't work
? Will she work?
I will help you with that.
It will rain tomorrow.
tomorrownext yearsoonin the future
10
Future — be going to
S + am/is/are + going to + verb
Planos já decididos e previsões baseadas em evidência presente.
+ She is going to work
− She isn't going to work
? Is she going to work?
I am going to study tonight.
Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.
tonightnext weeksoon
11
Future Continuous
S + will + be + verb-ing
Ação que estará em progresso em determinado momento no futuro.
+ She will be working
− She won't be working
? Will she be working?
At 8pm, I will be eating dinner.
This time next year, I'll be traveling.
at this time tomorrowby thenthis time next year
12
Future Perfect
S + will + have + past participle
Ação que estará concluída antes de determinado momento no futuro.
+ She will have worked
− She won't have worked
? Will she have worked?
By 2030, I will have graduated.
She will have finished by noon.
byby the timebefore
