Past Perfect Continuous

Passado Perfeito Contínuo — ação em andamento antes de outra no passado

Quando usar?

  • Ação contínua que acontecia antes de outro evento no passado: "I had been waiting for 30 minutes when the bus arrived"
  • Explicar causa de algo no passado: "She was tired because she had been working all day"
  • Enfatizar a duração de algo que vinha acontecendo até certo ponto do passado

Estrutura

FormaEstruturaExemplo
Afirmativa Sujeito + had been + verbo-ing I had been running for an hour.
Negativa Sujeito + had not been + verbo-ing He hadn't been paying attention.
Interrogativa Had + sujeito + been + verbo-ing? Had they been waiting long?

Exemplos com tradução

I had been studying for 3 hours when she called.

Eu estava estudando há 3 horas quando ela ligou.

They had been living in London before they moved to Paris.

Eles estavam morando em Londres antes de se mudarem para Paris.

She was tired because she had been working all day.

Ela estava cansada porque tinha trabalhado / vinha trabalhando o dia todo.

The ground was wet. It had been raining.

O chão estava molhado. Tinha chovido / Estivera chovendo.

Had you been waiting long before the taxi came?

Você estava esperando há muito tempo antes do táxi chegar?

Past Perfect vs Past Perfect Continuous

Past PerfectPast Perfect Continuous
Foco na ação completa Foco na duração / processo
I had read the book. (terminei) I had been reading for hours. (ênfase no tempo)
