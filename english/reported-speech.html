Reported Speech

Relatar o que alguém disse sem usar aspas

O que é?

  • Relatar o que alguém disse, sem usar as palavras exatas (sem aspas)
  • Direct: She said, "I am tired."
  • Reported: She said that she was tired.
  • O tempo verbal recua um passo no passado

Backshift — recuo dos tempos verbais

Discurso DiretoDiscurso Indireto
Present Simple → "I work here."Past Simple → she said she worked there.
Present Continuous → "I am leaving."Past Continuous → he said he was leaving.
Past Simple → "She called."Past Perfect → he said she had called.
Present Perfect → "I have finished."Past Perfect → she said she had finished.
will → "I will help."would → she said she would help.
can → "I can do it."could → he said he could do it.

Verbos de reporte

  • say → não especifica a quem: She said (that) she was tired.
  • tell → especifica a quem: She told me (that) she was tired.
  • ask → para perguntas: He asked if I was okay.
  • Outros: explain, admit, promise, warn, suggest, remind, offer

Perguntas em Reported Speech

  • Perguntas viram afirmativas (ordem sujeito + verbo)
  • Use if / whether para yes/no questions
  • Use a WH word para WH questions

Direct: "Are you coming?" → Reported: He asked if I was coming.

Ele perguntou se eu estava indo.

Direct: "Where do you live?" → Reported: She asked where I lived.

Ela perguntou onde eu morava.
